# RISKX — Non-Commercial Use Policy

RISKX is released as an **open-source, non-commercial** tool. This policy works
together with the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) file (Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International) to define exactly
what users of this project may and may not do.

## Permitted use (free)

Any individual, research group, educational institution, non-profit
organization, or private security researcher may use, build, and run RISKX
without charge, including for the following purposes:

- personal and professional learning in cyber security
- academic research and coursework
- non-profit and community security research
- securing your own infrastructure, home lab, or personal projects
- sharing the unmodified tool and its documentation with others (with
  attribution to the author, as the license requires)

## Not permitted (commercial use)

Commercial use is expressly prohibited without a separate written agreement
from the author. "Commercial use" includes, but is not limited to:

- selling the tool, binaries, or packaged versions of it
- using it to provide a paid security service, consulting engagement, or
  managed offering to clients
- embedding it in a commercial product or service
- monetized redistribution of any part of the project

Anyone who wishes to use RISKX commercially must obtain a commercial license
from the author directly.

## Responsible use

RISKX performs network observation. Users must only scan systems they own or
have explicit written permission to test, and must comply with all applicable
laws and regulations in their jurisdiction. The author provides the software
as-is, without warranty of any kind (see `LICENSE`, Section 5).

## Why non-commercial

RISKX was created by a cyber security researcher for the benefit of the
security community. The non-commercial restriction protects the community
from having free research work resold, while still allowing everyone to
learn, research, and defend freely.
